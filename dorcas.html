A letter for you

To My Dear Dorcas, A Lady Most Worthy of Quill and Parchment ✍️❤️,

“If time be a thief that steals our hours, let it not deceive thee into thinking it hath stolen my affection; for though my days be occupied, my heart hath not declared thee a stranger.”

My dearest Adedolapo,

Who would have thought that a meeting in church would lead to a friendship like ours? What began as simple conversations has grown into shared meals, random gist, countless memories, playful insults, and a friendship in which I can spend your money with absolutely no remorse. 😂

Somewhere between all these little things, you became someone genuinely special to me. And I don’t think I say that enough.

One thing I admire about you is how you handle the different parts of your life. The way you take your business seriously, your work as an educator, and your walk with God are things I respect deeply. I love that beyond the fun we have together, there is a woman with purpose, ambition, and a desire to keep growing. I see that in you, Dorcas, and I hope you never lose it.

Now, about this accusation of nonchalance. 😂

I know I haven’t been reaching out as much lately, and I understand why you have noticed. My workload has been demanding a lot of me, and sometimes I get so caught up trying to keep everything together that I fall short in keeping up with the people who matter to me.

But, Dorcas, being busy with my life is not the same as being tired of having you in it.

“Mistake not the silence of a friend for the silence of a heart; for even when words grow few, affection need not grow cold.”

You still have a special place in my heart. I haven’t forgotten our friendship, the memories we have made, or how naturally we have grown close. I know that care should not only be felt but shown, and I want to do better at that. I don’t want you to have to wonder whether you still matter to me because my schedule has become overwhelming.

And please, let us not become those friends who eventually say, “Remember when we used to spend time together?” We still have plenty of food to eat, gist to gist, memories to create, and insults to exchange. 😂 We have a friendship to maintain!

And as for your decision to make me one of your chief bridesmaids, I shall accept this appointment with great honour. 😂❤️ But jokes aside, it means a lot to know that you want me beside you on such an important day. I hope we continue to share enough beautiful moments that, when that day comes, we will have plenty of memories to smile about.

My prayer for you is that God continues to bless your business, strengthen you in your work, and deepen your relationship with Him. May the things you are quietly building become beautiful testimonies, and may you always have the strength and wisdom to keep becoming the woman you desire to be.

Dorcas, I am genuinely grateful that we met. Grateful for the laughter, the conversations, the foods, the friendship, and even the small, silly things that have become part of our story.

“Though seasons change and daily duties call, some people remain dear through it all.”

And you, my friend, remain dear to me.

With love, plenty of mischief, and the highest regard,

your chief bridesmaid. ❤️😂
